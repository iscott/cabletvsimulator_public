THE 90'S ARE BACK!

Cable TV Simulator is an unofficial fan project for Apple TV that simulates the 90's cable TV experience. No pausing, no skipping. You just choose a channel and watch, vintage commercials and all.

  • 35 channels that are always live, from Saturday morning cartoons to late-night public access
  • Vintage commercial breaks between shows
  • A blue-and-yellow channel guide with the current show playing in the corner
  • Channel up and down with the remote, just like a cable box

All videos stream directly from the Internet Archive (archive.org). This app isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Internet Archive or Spelunkr.tv, but we greatly appreciate their work, so please consider donating to the Internet Archive and Spelunkr.tv.
