Using the remote

  • ▲ / ▼, or CH+ / CH−: change the channel (hold to keep flipping)
  • ◀ / ▶ or Play/Pause: show what's on now
  • Select: open the channel guide, and tune to the highlighted channel
  • Back: close the guide, or return to the Apple TV home screen

Common questions

  • The screen says "Tuning in" for a while. Videos stream from the Internet Archive, and some tapes take several seconds to start. Long tapes can take up to half a minute.
  • I see "Technical Difficulties". That tape couldn't be loaded from the Internet Archive. The channel comes back on its own with the next program, or you can change channels.
  • I see "No Signal". Your Apple TV isn't connected to the internet. The picture comes back when the connection does.
  • Can I pause or rewind? No. Like real cable, every channel is live.
