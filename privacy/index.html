PRIVACY POLICY

Cable TV Simulator for Apple TV · Last updated November 10, 2026

Cable TV Simulator does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

What the app stores

The app saves two settings, only on your Apple TV: the last channel you watched, and that you agreed to the welcome notice. These never leave your device, and deleting the app removes them.

What the app does not do

There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking of any kind. The developer receives no data about you or your viewing.

Video streaming

All videos stream directly from the Internet Archive (archive.org). When you watch a channel, your Apple TV connects to archive.org to play the video, and the Internet Archive may receive standard connection information such as your IP address, as with any website. See the Internet Archive's terms and privacy policy.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
